Privacy Policy

Walking Together+

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Walking Together+ helps people track walking activity, work toward personal goals, and optionally share walking experiences with a linked partner. We respect your privacy and want you to understand how information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the app processes:

  • Name, email address, and account credentials.
  • Walking goals, step counts, and activity dates.
  • Manual or device-provided step tracking data.
  • Walking sessions, duration, distance, and progress.
  • Profile settings, including optional physical measurements used for activity estimates.
  • Partner connections, invitations, and sharing settings.
  • Memories, messages, high-fives, and achievements.
  • Subscription status and purchase-related event data.
  • Sign-in and invitation-attempt information used to protect accounts.

Account passwords are stored using password hashes, rather than as readable passwords.

2. How We Use Information

We process information to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts.
  • Track walking progress and personal goals.
  • Provide optional partner-sharing features.
  • Display memories, achievements, and journeys.
  • Manage Premium subscription access.
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse.
  • Respond to support inquiries.

3. Partner Sharing

Users may voluntarily connect with another user. Linked partners can participate in shared activities, goals, and progress features.

Step-sharing preferences can be managed through the app's Account & privacy settings. Some joint records are associated with both partners.

4. Service Providers

Walking Together+ uses service providers to operate its backend, store application data, and manage subscriptions. These may include MongoDB Atlas, Render, RevenueCat, Apple, and Google, depending on the services used.

Subscription payments are processed through the applicable app marketplace. The app's backend processes subscription status rather than full payment-card details.

5. Data Security

We use authentication, password hashing, access controls, and other security measures designed to protect user information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Account information is retained as needed to operate the app. Users can request account deletion through the Account & privacy section of the app or by contacting support.

Account deletion is designed to remove the account and its associated activity data from the active application database. Shared couple records may also be removed, while the surviving partner's own walking history is preserved.

Certain subscription-event metadata may remain after the associated app account identifier is removed. Service-provider records and backups may follow separate retention requirements.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You can update supported profile settings, manage step sharing, unlink a partner, and request deletion of your account.

For questions about accessing, correcting, or deleting your information, contact our support team.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
